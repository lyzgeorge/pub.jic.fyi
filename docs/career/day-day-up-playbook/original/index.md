# The Day Day Up Career Playbook in Action - Original Edition

### A practice-oriented lecture textbook

*Compiled 2026-09-23 from 270 transcribed videos (YouTube @MrJonathanCareer, Bilibili and Xiaohongshu channels; ~188,000 words of speech). Every statement in this book is drawn from those transcripts; nothing has been added from outside the corpus.*

## How to use this book

1. **Read a chapter as a lecture.** Each numbered section moves from concept → the coach's reasoning (usually the interviewer's or manager's point of view) → a step-by-step method → a worked script quoted from the videos → the mistakes the coach warns about.
2. **Do the practice set before moving on.** The drills are written to be *done*, not read: write the answer out, record yourself, run the role-play with a friend, rewrite the before/after. The fill-in templates are the coach's scripts with your details left blank.
3. **Use the cheat sheets for rehearsal.** Each chapter ends with a one-screen summary of its frameworks, formulas and phrases - print them the night before an interview or a 1:1.
4. **Suggested path for a job search:** Chapters 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5. **Once employed:** 6 → 7 → 8, with 9 as the through-line.

Where two videos disagree (the coach's advice evolved between 2018 and 2026), the text says so.
