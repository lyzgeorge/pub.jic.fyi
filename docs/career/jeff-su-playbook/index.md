# Jeff Su's Career Playbook

A practical guide synthesized from Jeff Su's videos in Chapters 1–9, followed by a product-building chapter based on Michael Seibel's Y Combinator lecture. Related career advice is grouped by concept, and points that changed across Jeff's videos are explained in context. His examples reflect experience in consulting and technology, mainly in US and Greater China settings. Adapt them to your role and location, and use only facts and achievements that are true for you.

Start with Chapter 1 to understand what employers assess. Chapters 2–3 cover LinkedIn, Chapters 4–7 cover interviews, Chapter 8 shows how to use AI during preparation, and Chapter 9 covers resourcefulness at work. Chapter 10 covers finding a product problem, building an MVP, measuring use, and iterating. Each chapter ends with a check you can apply to your own work.

The book is a synthesis of the linked [source videos](./sources.md). Chapter 10 has its own Y Combinator source and addresses founders; its career applications are analogies. Dates, tool features, and statistics quoted in the videos may have changed; where a source did not substantiate a statistic, the chapter says so. Editorial guidance is identified separately from the speakers' advice.
