# pub.jic.fyi

A public knowledge notebook for useful context, practical references, and carefully sourced notes.

**JIC** means **just in case**. **FYI** means **for your information**. Together, `pub.jic.fyi` is a small public archive for things worth keeping close at hand.

## Sources

This site is a knowledge notebook, not an authority. For decisions involving schools, governments, employers, healthcare, immigration, law, finance, or other time-sensitive topics, check the original source before acting.

## Notes

- [TCD Postgraduate Orientation 2026](./tcd/postgraduate/orientation-2026/index.md) - reference notes from Trinity College Dublin's 2026 postgraduate orientation sessions, grouped by task: registration, study, money, wellbeing, and community.
- [The Day Day Up Career Playbook in Action](./career/day-day-up-playbook/index.md) - nine chapters on job search, workplace development, and career strategy, adapted from Jonathan Lee.
- [Jeff Su's Career Playbook](./career/jeff-su-playbook/index.md) - ten chapters on careers and product building, drawing on Jeff Su's videos and a Y Combinator lecture by Michael Seibel.
