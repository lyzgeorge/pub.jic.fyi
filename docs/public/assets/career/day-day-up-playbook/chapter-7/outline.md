# Chapter 7 illustration outline

## Illustration 1
**Position**: 7.1, after the four-topic table
**Purpose**: Show how a 1:1 connects expectations, priorities, resources, and development to an agreed next action
**Visual Content**: Four rounded cards in a 2×2 grid around a small central circle labeled "1:1". Cards: "Expectations" with target icon and small "Success?"; "Priorities" with ordered list icon and "What first?"; "Resources" with two branching paths and "A or B?"; "Development" with upward arrow and "Grow next?". Four arrows point inward from cards, one arrow points downward to a bottom outcome bar labeled "Next action · Owner · Outcome".
