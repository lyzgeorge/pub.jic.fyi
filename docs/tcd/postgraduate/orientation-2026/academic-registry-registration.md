# Registration and the Academic Registry

> **Original title:** Let's Talk About... Registration with the Academic Registry
>
> **Session date:** 9 September 2026
>
> **Source:** [Panopto recording](https://tcd.cloud.panopto.eu/Panopto/Pages/Viewer.aspx?id=4a0416fc-300b-430f-a5d0-b4ba00b42e4b)

The Academic Registry supports the student lifecycle from application to graduation. For a new postgraduate student, its most immediate roles are registration, student records, official documents, ID cards, fees and administrative enquiries.

## Complete every registration task

Registration takes place in **my.tcd.ie** through eight tasks. A fee payment alone does not finish the financial task, and completing only some steps does not make a student fully registered.

![Eight-step Trinity registration process covering personal, contact, emergency, course, survey, financial, and terms tasks](/assets/tcd/postgraduate/orientation-2026/academic-registry-registration/registration-process.jpg)

The session gave **6 October 2026** as the general deadline. Treat this as recording-specific and check the current portal. Outstanding Garda vetting, funding or scholarship records, and departmental waivers can block completion. Do not ignore portal reminders; if a blocker remains near the deadline, submit an enquiry through **ask.tcd.ie** and explain it.

![my.tcd.ie registration task list, where incomplete items show a red X and completed items a green tick](/assets/tcd/postgraduate/orientation-2026/academic-registry-registration/registration-tasks.jpg)

A reliable check is simple: every task must show completion, including financial details and terms and conditions. Registration unlocks services such as the student card, library, buildings, and sport access.

## Documents and the student record

The proof-of-registration letter in my.tcd.ie can support bank, PPSN, IRP, and address applications. Academic Registry can also validate student-status forms and provide relevant transcripts, student-history letters, and government-funding forms. Keep copies because the same evidence may later be useful for visas, internships, or other applications.

Students can update home and term addresses and contact numbers through **Maintain Student Record**. Changes to name, gender, date of birth, or nationality require Academic Registry, a Change of Personal Details Form, and official evidence. After a name change is processed, the session says a replacement student card can be issued without charge. Student information cannot be discussed with a third party without the student's permission.

## Obtain and use the TCard

After full registration, new entrants receive portal instructions to upload a passport-style image through the TCard service. The recording directed students to collect the card from the Orientation Hub 48 hours later with physical identification; locations and dates are time-sensitive and should be checked in current instructions. Returning Trinity students moving to postgraduate study may need Academic Registry help to change the existing photograph.

The card enables relevant building, library, gym, and other access. The recording states that a lost-card replacement at the Service Desk costs €20; verify the current fee.

## Route problems to the right place

Use Academic Registry for registration, records, documents, cards, finance administration, examinations, graduation, and general administrative guidance. For withdrawal, illness, missed examinations, or programme difficulties, contact the course administrator or supervisor promptly; they can engage the Student Cases team or school. Other needs may belong with Student Learning Development, Disability Service, Counselling, or the Students' Union.

## Practical checklist

1. Read Trinity email and my.tcd.ie intray messages regularly.
2. Complete all eight registration tasks and confirm every status.
3. Resolve vetting, funding, scholarship, or waiver blockers early.
4. Download and retain proof of registration and other official documents.
5. Check the student record and use the formal route for protected personal details.
6. Follow current TCard instructions only after registration is complete.
7. Ask early: use ask.tcd.ie or the Academic Registry Service Desk in the Watts Building.
