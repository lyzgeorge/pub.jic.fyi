## International student support

### Trinity Global Room

The **Trinity Global Room** is the support and community hub for international students. More than 40 international and Irish student ambassadors provide peer advice, service referrals, and social support. During term, it is open Monday to Friday, 09:00–21:00.

The Global Room is on the first floor of the Watts Building, beside Academic Registry.

![Campus map showing the Trinity Global Room beside the Watts Building on the east side of College Park.](./assets/international-student-orientation/global-room-map.jpg)

*The Global Room is marked beside the Watts Building, near Westland Row and Academic Registry.*

It also runs immigration clinics, orientation events, cultural events, and information sessions. Contact `tcdglobalroom@tcd.ie`; current events and clinic times are published through `@tcdglobalroom`.

## Immigration registration

**Immigration registration** establishes permission to remain in Ireland for the duration of study. Students who are not citizens of the EU, EEA, United Kingdom, or Switzerland generally register with Immigration Service Delivery (ISD) before their entry permission expires, commonly within 90 days of arrival.

### Stamp 2 and Stamp 2A

| Permission | Typical student in the session | Employment | Core distinction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Stamp 2** | A student enrolled in a full-time degree, including a full-time master's programme | Up to 20 hours per week during term and 40 hours during designated holiday periods, subject to current rules | Intended for full-time study and permits limited casual employment |
| **Stamp 2A** | An exchange or visiting student attending for one semester | Not presented as carrying the Stamp 2 employment permission | A different study category with different conditions and evidence |

A one-year full-time degree remains a full-time degree for this classification; it is not the one-semester exchange category.

### Registration workflow

1. Book through the ISD portal for a date after the official course start date. Identity verification may use ID-Pal.
2. Generate the proof of registration within one week of the appointment.
3. Attend the Burgh Quay Registration Office appointment with the complete document set.
4. Pay the stated €300 fee with a physical debit or credit card; cash and contactless payment are not accepted.

### Evidence required

| Evidence | Purpose and important detail |
| --- | --- |
| Valid passport | Identity and current entry permission |
| Proof of registration | Confirms active enrolment; generate it from `my.tcd.ie` within one week of the appointment |
| Proof of fees paid | Often appears in the same Trinity letter as proof of registration |
| Proof of finances | Required for non-visa-required nationals; the amount depends on course duration and current ISD rules |
| Private health insurance | Must cover the relevant period of residence and satisfy ISD requirements |
| Physical debit or credit card | Payment of the registration fee; cash and contactless payment are excluded |

In `my.tcd.ie`, the proof of registration is generated through **Registration → Confirmation of Registration** and then retrieved from the portal intray.

### Irish Residence Permit

The **Irish Residence Permit (IRP)** records the permission type and expiry date. It is posted to the address confirmed at the appointment. If it has not arrived within 15 working days, submit a query through the ISD customer-service portal. Employment permission ends when the underlying immigration permission expires.

## Travel outside Ireland

- **Schengen Area:** Ireland is an EU member but is not part of the Schengen Area. A student may need a Schengen visa for short visits to participating countries.
- **United Kingdom and Northern Ireland:** An IRP does not by itself authorize entry. Depending on nationality and purpose, a visa or Electronic Travel Authorisation may be required.
- **Application evidence:** Embassies set their own requirements. Some request confirmed transport and accommodation, while others advise applicants not to purchase them before a decision.

Check requirements with the destination's embassy or immigration authority and allow time for scarce appointment slots.

## Banking, work, and public services

| System | Purpose | When it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Irish bank account | Receives wages and supports local payments or direct debits | Useful for students working in Ireland or staying long enough to need local payment infrastructure; it is not required for immigration registration |
| PPS number | Identifies a person for tax, employment, and public-service administration | Usually needed when working and may also be requested for other public services |

Bank requirements vary. Common evidence includes a passport, proof of an Irish address, proof of student status, and sometimes a home-country bank statement or IRP. The acceptable account also depends on the employer's payroll arrangements.

### PPS number

The **Personal Public Service number (PPSN)** identifies a person across employment, tax, social welfare, and some public services. Apply through MyWelfare after creating a MyGovID account. Typical evidence includes identity, an Irish address, and the reason for applying. A current Trinity registration letter can support student status and address; cite employment only when supported by a job offer or equivalent evidence.

## Health insurance

Non-EU students are required to maintain private medical insurance that meets immigration requirements. Coverage should extend across the full period of residence and include emergency treatment and hospitalization.

First-year students may use insurance purchased in their home country if it satisfies ISD requirements. Providers listed in the session include Irish Life Health, VHI, Laya Healthcare, and Study & Protect.

## Safety and scam resistance

### SafeZone

**SafeZone** is Trinity's campus safety application. It shares the user's location with campus security when an alert is raised and provides routes for first aid, non-urgent help, and emergencies. It also includes a campus map.

### Scam pattern

Accommodation, banking, and immigration scams often create artificial urgency. Respond by:

1. Pause the transaction or conversation.
2. Verify the website domain and the claimed organization.
3. Find an official contact number independently rather than using the number supplied in the message.
4. Never disclose a password, verification code, or banking credential by phone.

## Cultural adaptation

### Global dexterity

**Global dexterity** is the ability to adapt behaviour across cultural contexts without abandoning personal values or identity. Adaptation may alternate between confidence, confusion, frustration, and homesickness.

### Visible and invisible culture

| Visible culture | Less visible culture |
| --- | --- |
| Food, clothing, music, public celebrations, and signage | Communication style, disagreement, attitudes to authority, expectations of formality, and academic norms |

### Academic and everyday communication

Trinity's conversational informality does not remove academic roles. A senior academic may introduce themselves by first name, while the relationship still requires professional respect.

Irish communication may be indirect. Phrases such as “That's interesting” or “I'll see what I can do” can function as a softened refusal rather than a commitment.

When regional accents or Irish-influenced English affect understanding, ask for repetition, slower speech, or rephrasing immediately, especially in academic meetings.

### Settling in

Build a routine in Irish local time, join clubs or societies, attend Global Room events, and maintain contact with home without replacing daily life in Ireland.

## Practical sequence

| Stage | Priority |
| --- | --- |
| Arrival | Update the Irish address in Trinity systems, locate the Global Room, and configure SafeZone |
| Before immigration registration | Confirm the correct permission category, book through the ISD portal, and generate current evidence |
| After the appointment | Check the IRP details and delivery; escalate through ISD if it does not arrive within the stated period |
| Before employment | Confirm Stamp 2 work permission, then arrange payroll-compatible banking and a PPSN |
| Before international travel | Check the destination's entry rules independently of Irish residence permission |
| During the academic term | Clarify unfamiliar language early and build a local academic and social support network |
