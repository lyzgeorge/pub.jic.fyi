# Working While Studying at Trinity

> **Original title:** Let's Talk About... Working in Trinity
>
> **Session date:** 8 September 2026
>
> **Source:** [Panopto recording](https://tcd.cloud.panopto.eu/Panopto/Pages/Viewer.aspx?id=3fb86ed0-1cf2-4cb4-973a-b4ba00ad7f52)

Working during postgraduate study involves three separate questions: whether immigration permission allows the work, whether tax and payroll records are ready, and whether the hours remain compatible with study. Rules and rates change, so figures quoted in the recording should be checked against current Irish government and Trinity guidance before acting.

## Before accepting work

A worker normally needs a **PPS number** for Irish public services and tax administration, and must register employment details with Revenue. Delayed setup can trigger emergency taxation until records are corrected. Employers also need bank details; the session recommends arranging an account that the employer can pay and keeping proof of identity and address available.

Immigration permission determines whether a non-EU student may work and for how many hours. The recording distinguishes Stamp 2 from Stamp 2A and notes separate term-time and designated-holiday limits. Because immigration periods and conditions can change, verify the current wording on official Immigration Service Delivery material rather than relying on the slide alone. EU/EEA status follows different rules.

## Understand employment terms

Obtain written information about the employer, role, pay, expected hours, and contract duration. Check the current statutory minimum wage, break entitlements, annual leave, sick-pay provisions, and any sector-specific rules through official sources. Tips are not a substitute for wages. A union can provide collective representation and individual support where one exists.

Keep copies of contracts, schedules, payslips, submitted forms, and correspondence. These records are useful for correcting pay or tax errors and for immigration or administrative evidence.

## Trinity casual-work setup

A Trinity student taking occasional work also becomes an employee in the relevant payroll process. The session describes an **Occasional Staff Engagement Form** completed with the manager. A separate engagement may be required for each manager or budget even when the student already has another Trinity role.

After HR setup, the worker receives a staff identifier distinct from the student number. Hours must then be claimed using the current payroll process. Forms require the employee’s signature and identifying information; missing fields or late manager approval can defer payment. Pay categories and rates are centrally defined, so confirm the category with the manager rather than entering a self-selected rate.

## Where to find opportunities

![Slide listing sources of student work at Trinity, including teaching, the Library, invigilation, ambassador work, the Students’ Union, newsletters, and MyCareer](/assets/tcd/postgraduate/orientation-2026/working-in-trinity/job-sources.jpg)

Possible routes mentioned in the session include:

- teaching, tutorials, demonstrating, grading, or research work arranged through a School;
- Library shelving or stacks work;
- exam invigilation through Academic Registry;
- Global Room ambassador roles;
- Students’ Union jobs;
- Student Learning Development writing-group facilitation;
- short projects such as tours, focus groups, surveys, and event promotion;
- vacancies and internships advertised through MyCareer and postgraduate newsletters.

Many roles are advertised by email or within a department. Building a professional relationship with lecturers and supervisors helps students hear about suitable academic work, but payment and expectations must be clarified before accepting it.

## Protect study and immigration limits

Multiple jobs may be possible, but total working hours—not hours per employer—must remain within any immigration restriction. More than one employer can also complicate tax allocation. Students should monitor Revenue records and review whether tax was overpaid.

Before taking a role, compare its timetable with classes, laboratory work, supervision, and deadlines. Flexible campus work can be useful, but the academic programme remains the primary basis of student immigration permission.

## Checklist

1. Verify current permission to work and maximum hours.
2. Arrange PPS, Revenue, bank, and immigration records through official channels.
3. Obtain written terms and confirm the pay category.
4. Complete Trinity engagement paperwork for each relevant manager.
5. Submit accurate hours early enough for approval and retain a copy.
6. Check payslips, tax, leave, and statutory rights.
7. Search School channels, MyCareer, Library, Academic Registry, Global Room, union, and postgraduate newsletters.
