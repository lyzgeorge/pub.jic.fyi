## Illustration 1
**Position**: Chapter 9, section 9.5 “The garden model: seed, soil, climate” (after the opening explanation and before the detailed layer subsections)
**Purpose**: Make the three diagnostic layers memorable and show that career growth depends on adjusting the right layer rather than blaming one’s ability for every problem.
**Visual Content**: A single career garden with three labeled layers: SEED = skills and interests; SOIL = manager, company, team; CLIMATE = industry, geography, timing. A hand-drawn arrow at the bottom says “Adjust one layer at a time.” English labels only so the same asset can be reused in English, Chinese, and Spanish pages.
